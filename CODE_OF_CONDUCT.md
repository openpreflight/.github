# Code of Conduct

This applies to every repository under the
[openpreflight](https://github.com/openpreflight) organization — issues, pull
requests, discussions, and commit messages.

## The short version

Be straightforward and be civil. Argue about the code, not the person.

## Expected

- Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Keep criticism about the change: what it breaks, what it costs, what it
  misses.
- Accept that "no" is a legitimate answer to a proposal, and that a maintainer
  may not have time for yours.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, harassment, or sustained hostility.
- Demeaning remarks about anyone's identity — including race, gender, gender
  identity, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, age, nationality, or
  religion.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Deliberately derailing threads, or reopening a settled decision to relitigate
  it.

## Reporting

Email **security@openpreflight.xyz**. Reports are read by the maintainer only.
Include links to the relevant threads. You will get a reply.

Do not open a public issue about someone's conduct — that makes it worse for
everyone, including you.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close threads, and block accounts,
without a warning step if the behaviour is severe enough. There is no appeals
process; this is a small project, not a court.

## Scope

This covers activity in the organization's repositories, and off-platform
behaviour directed at people because of their participation here.
